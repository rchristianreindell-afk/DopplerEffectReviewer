Each circle starts where the source emitted a sound pulse and expands outward. Crowded wavefronts mean shorter wavelength; spread-out wavefronts mean longer wavelength.

4. Formula and substitution

fL = fS × ((V + VL) / (V − VS))
fL = 500 × ((343 + 20) / (343 − 30))
fL = 579.87 Hz

Symbols: fL = observed/listener frequency (Hz), fS = source frequency (Hz), V = speed of sound (m/s), VL = listener speed (m/s), VS = source speed (m/s). This model assumes straight-line motion and a still air medium.
